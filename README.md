# Dementia

Reminds you of the things you keep forgetting. Made for World of Warcraft: Forever.

## Features

- **Mining Tracking** – turns Find Minerals back on after you log in or get revived.
- **Sleeping Bag Reminder** – yawns at you every 30 seconds when you don't have the full 3 stacks of Well Rested.
- **Buff Food Reminder** – plays a stomach grumble when you're not Well Fed.

The reminders stay quiet in cities and inns, in combat, on flight paths and while you're AFK.

## Settings

Everything is off by default. Turn on what you want in **Options → AddOns → Dementia**.
