# Dementia

Reminds you of the things you keep forgetting. Made for World of Warcraft: Forever.

## Features

- **Restore Gathering Tracking** - turns Find Minerals and Find Herbs back on after you log in or get revived.
- **Sleeping Bag Reminder** - yawns at you when Well Rested is missing, under 3 stacks, or about to run out.
- **Buff Food Reminder** - sighs at you when you're not Well Fed.
- **Pick your sound** - choose a yawn and a sigh from any race, or leave it on Random.
- **Reminder Interval** - set how often the reminders repeat (30 seconds to 10 minutes).
- **Questie Turn-in Highlight** - makes Questie's turn-in icons on the world map bigger and paints them a color of your choice, so you stop forgetting to turn in quests.

The reminders stay quiet in cities and inns, in combat, while dead, on flight paths, while AFK and in addon-restricted areas. After combat, a resurrection or logging in, they wait a few seconds before nagging again.

## Settings

Everything is off by default. Turn on what you want in **Options → AddOns → Dementia**. The Questie option is greyed out if Questie isn't loaded.
